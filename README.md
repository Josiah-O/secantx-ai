## What's live

Screen-aware research: The Vision desktop app reads your trading screen to ground responses in what you're actually looking at.

Real-time analysis: Multi-agent reasoning with smooth, token-by-token streaming so you get feedback immediately.

Deep live data sources: Crypto (CEX/DEX), equities, DeFi TVL/revenue, macro, and news/sentiment. Real-time coverage across major venues.

Trust & safety: Premium/credits gating with non-custodial design (users sign their own actions).
